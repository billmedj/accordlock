# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this repository a respectful, safe, and
welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that supports a healthy community include:

- showing empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive technical feedback;
- focusing disagreement on evidence, decisions, and impact;
- taking responsibility, apologizing when appropriate, and repairing harm;
- respecting privacy, confidentiality, and clearly stated boundaries; and
- acting in the best interests of the repository and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, personal attacks, trolling, deliberate intimidation, impersonation,
publication of private information, or other conduct that a reasonable person
would consider inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This Code applies in repository issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews,
security-reporting channels, and other spaces where a person officially
represents this repository.

## Reporting

Do not publish sensitive conduct reports or personal information in a public
issue. Report platform-hosted abuse with GitHub's content-reporting tools. Other
conduct concerns should be sent through any private maintainer contact method
configured in the repository settings. The maintainers designated in those
settings are responsible for handling reports.

If no private maintainer contact is configured, do not invent a contact or reuse
one from another project. Use GitHub's content-reporting process and ask a
repository administrator to configure an appropriate private channel.

Maintainers will protect reporter confidentiality as far as reasonably possible,
avoid conflicts of interest, and apply proportionate corrective action. A
maintainer who is the subject of a report must not participate in deciding it.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and context, maintainers may issue a correction or
warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove access to
community spaces under their control. Decisions should document the violated
standard and any conditions for return without exposing confidential details.

Retaliation against a person who makes a good-faith report or assists an
investigation is prohibited.
